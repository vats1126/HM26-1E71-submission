# Presentation — 10-Slide Template (5% of total score)

[← Back to README](../README.md)

> **Output:** PDF, max **10 slides**, 16:9, named `<TeamID>_presentation.pdf`, uploaded to Google Drive and linked in [`resource.md`](../resource.md).
> **Purpose:** A quick reference for judges. It mirrors your Part 1 pitch; it doesn't replace it.
> **Rule of thumb:** ≤ 30 words per slide (excluding diagrams). Screenshots of your *real* product, not mock-ups.

---

| # | Slide | What goes on it | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title** | Project name, tagline, Team name + ID, member names & colleges, sub-problem tag | The tagline should say what it does, not what it's called. |
| 2 | **The Problem** | Chosen sub-problem; 2–3 concrete Mysuru facts or scenarios; who is hurt today | One real-feeling scenario beats three statistics. |
| 3 | **Target Users** | 2–3 personas (citizen, officer, field worker) with context: device, connectivity, language | Show you thought about who *can't* use a normal app. |
| 4 | **Our Solution** | Core flow as 4–5 steps or a simple journey graphic; 1 hero screenshot | Must match what the video demo shows. |
| 5 | **Architecture** | Same diagram as [`docs/architecture.md`](../docs/architecture.md); label components and data flow | Keep it readable at a glance. No 40-box diagrams. |
| 6 | **Hard Constraints** | Condensed [`docs/constraints.md`](../docs/constraints.md). Table: fake reports · jurisdiction · priority · bad input · offline → our approach for each | Mark honestly: ✅ handled / ⚠️ partial / ❌ not yet. |
| 7 | **Key Decision** | Chosen approach vs rejected alternative, 3–4 row comparison, cost accepted | A condensed [Decision Log](./decision-log-template.md) Q1 + Q2. |
| 8 | **Proof It Works** | 2–4 real screenshots: normal flow, a rejected/flagged input, offline state | Caption each with what it proves. |
| 9 | **Limits & Scale** | Top 3 things that break at city scale + fix for each; known gaps | Condensed [Decision Log](./decision-log-template.md) Q3. |
| 10 | **What's Next & Disclosure** | 3 next steps (e.g. pilot with 1 ward, GIS data, Kannada voice input); one line on AI tools used (full detail in [`ai.md`](../ai.md)); links: live MVP, repo | End with the live URL. |

---

### Design checklist

- [ ] ≤ 10 slides, exported to PDF (not PPTX / Google Slides link).
- [ ] Minimum 18 pt body text; high-contrast colours.
- [ ] No lorem ipsum, stock "smart city" imagery, or mock-ups of features that don't exist.
- [ ] Architecture diagram identical to [`docs/architecture.md`](../docs/architecture.md).
- [ ] AI tools named on slide 10 match [`ai.md`](../ai.md).
- [ ] File under ~20 MB (compress images if needed).
